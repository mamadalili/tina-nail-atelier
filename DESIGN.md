# Tina nail atelier

Persian RTL typography, a pearl-grey canvas, imperial green and burgundy connect the service catalogue, colour samples and booking form. Keep the owner's photographs distinct from illustrative manicure studies. See [image credits](docs/image-provenance.md).

## Booking

Keep the selected staff member, service, Jalali date and time together through the form and receipt. The calendar distinguishes open, occupied and closed slots using text as well as colour. On a phone the form stacks in reading order. Respect reduced motion and maintain a visible keyboard focus.
